\begin{abstract}
This archived note records a preliminary formulation and integrity checks for
geometric protection management on BED-BPP \emph{nominal} orders under
\emph{synthetic} dimensional settings. What was executed: synthetic contract
tests (G1), a deterministic known-growth harness preflight (eight episodes),
audits of that model, a tiny enumerated toy comparing analytical rules, and a
novelty review against primary sources. What was \emph{not} executed: a
stochastic G2 diagnosis, any new reinforcement-learning training, or an
empirical dimensional error law from BED-BPP. The study is closed for
insufficient scientific justification of a distinguishable contribution; it
does not claim novelty, superiority, RL necessity or uselessness in the
abstract, physical stability, or publication readiness.
\end{abstract}
