\section{Discussion and Limitations}

This repository note records a preliminary formulation and integrity checks, not a
submission-ready paper. G1 verified a software contract. The G2 v1 freeze used
deterministic known growth and is closed for reinforcement learning under
guarantee; its eight-episode preflight is harness evidence only. A synthetic
two-item enumeration showed that a weak local rule (\(\rho^\star=0.5\) then
minimise margin) is suboptimal, but prefer-guarantee, immediate expected
utility maximisation, and limited search under a declared arrival assumption all
select the support-covering margin and match the menu optimum. The toy chooser
is artificial (not the production extreme-point engine) and is not evidence about
that engine. Computing a support-covering margin is direct; future safe packing
is not automatically solved in every setting by knowing that margin---yet this
line did not exhibit a residual that justifies further experimental investment
against strong analytical baselines. Closest published antecedents already
treat conservative clearances (Wang--Hauser $\delta$ with linear reduction) and
fixed measurement buffers with an explicit reliability--compactness trade-off
(GOPT §IV-E). BED-BPP supplies nominal orders only; it is not a novelty claim
and does not provide a measured dimensional error law. Physical stability
remains unverified. Prior closed campaigns are unchanged and are not
reinterpreted as support for this line.
