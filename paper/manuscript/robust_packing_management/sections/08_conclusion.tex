\section{Conclusion}

This note closes the \texttt{robust\_packing\_management} experimental line for
lack of a concrete, distinguishable contribution relative to deterministic
clearance adaptation and fixed-buffer reliability trade-offs already documented
in primary sources. Remaining artefacts are formulation drafts and preliminary
evaluations. They do not claim that reinforcement learning succeeds, that it is
required, or that a paper is ready for submission.
