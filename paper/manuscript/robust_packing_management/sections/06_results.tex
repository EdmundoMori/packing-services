\section{Results}

No confirmatory packing-performance study is reported. Recorded artefacts are
preliminary: G1 unit/synthetic checks, a deterministic G2 v1 harness preflight,
and a tiny enumerated toy comparing protection rules. They do not establish
method superiority, reinforcement-learning necessity, industrial frequency, or
publication readiness. The line is closed for further experimental investment
pending a contribution that is not presently justified.
