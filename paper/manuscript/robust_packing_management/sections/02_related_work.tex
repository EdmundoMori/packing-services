\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Measurement error and fixed buffers.}
Yang et al.\ (GOPT) report real-robot packing in which camera-induced
measurement error can cause collisions; allocating a fixed $0.7\,\mathrm{cm}$
buffer around placed boxes yields average utilisation $67.5\%$ over 20 tests,
whereas a zero buffer raises utilisation on successful runs but produces
failures (2 of 20). They explicitly flag future work on reliability and
compactness (GOPT, Sec.~IV-E and Sec.~V; primary HTML source).

\paragraph{Industrial nominal orders.}
Kagerer et al.\ introduce BED-BPP, orders derived from grocery industry data,
with evaluation KPIs and a stability check. Their n-gram analysis uses a
$2\,\mathrm{cm}$ size tolerance, and they note that algorithms must account for
size tolerances; future work includes closing the simulation--reality gap
(BED-BPP, Sec.~3 and Sec.~6; primary PDF).

\paragraph{Conservative clearances and closed-loop correction.}
Wang and Hauser plan with a clearance threshold $\delta$ that penalises tight
fits and, when planning fails, reduce $\delta$ linearly toward zero; they also
study closed-loop vision replanning under execution uncertainty (IEEE TRO;
primary preprint verified).

\paragraph{Execution compliance under plural uncertainties.}
Shuai et al.\ analyse planning, transform, perception, and execution
uncertainty for robotic packing and emphasise compliant contact rather than a
learned geometric-margin manager (IET Control Theory Appl.; abstract and body
text verified; publisher epdf blocked in this environment).

\paragraph{Sequence robustness is a different uncertainty.}
Pan et al.\ (AR2L, NeurIPS 2023) train adjustable robust packing policies
against a permutation-based attacker on item sequences. That uncertainty class
is not interchangeable with dimensional geometry (primary NeurIPS PDF).

\paragraph{Other adjacent lines.}
One-dimensional online bin packing with size estimates, distribution-shift
adaptation for placement selection (ASAP), and robust offline one-dimensional
packing address related but non-identical problems. We do not claim global
absence of adaptive-margin learning elsewhere; unverified sources are marked as
such in the study archive.
