\section{Method}

\textit{G2 v1 freeze and deterministic preflight retained as historical harness
evidence. Stochastic uncertainty for management diagnosis remains pending
(design draft v2). No RL training.}

Protection $m$ from a small menu; envelope
\texttt{nominal\_oriented}+\texttt{margin\_oriented}; shared FLB.
Fixed placement chooser (\texttt{rank\_key}).

The frozen G2 v1 error map was
$\mathrm{realized}=\max(1,\mathrm{nominal}\cdot(1+\alpha))$ with public
scenario $\alpha$. That is a \emph{deterministic known-growth scenario}, not
unknown dimensional noise and not a POMDP: every method can compute the
realisation from public inputs before acting. Under that map, support-covering
margin $m_i=\alpha\cdot\mathrm{nominal}_i$ is an analytical guaranteed baseline;
learning a protection policy against it is trivial. The M3 v1 ``risk'' bit was
candidate existence under an envelope, not geometric failure probability.

A corrected stochastic sensitivity model
($R=N(1+\varepsilon)$ with public i.i.d.\ law, current $\varepsilon$ unobserved)
is drafted separately and is \emph{not} validated by the v1 preflight. BED-BPP
supplies nominal catalogue sizes, sequences, and targets only.
