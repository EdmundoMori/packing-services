\section*{Appendix: reuse summary}

Direct reuse targets include BED-BPP conversion helpers, the extreme-point online
session and mask, the three-rule proposal module, the present-only observation
encoder, and AABB audit utilities. The closed rule-selection environment is the
primary behavioural reference but must be wrapped so that truncation and corpus
export match schema v1 without rewriting historical campaign outputs. Excluded
as RL transitions: \(Q_{\hat{}}\) labels, production PPO checkpoints, and closed
evaluation captures. Full matrix:
\codepath{paper/studies/bed_bpp_rl/reuse_matrix.md}.
