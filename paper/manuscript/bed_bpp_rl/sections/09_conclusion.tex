\section{Conclusion}

BED-BPP-RL keeps BED-BPP and reinforcement learning as its study axes: an
interaction environment, a verifiable transition corpus, a pending minimal CPU
PPO demonstration on new experiences with train/eval separation, and a clear
record of what has actually been verified. R03-preparation completes offline
artifact contrast and publishes a measured real-preflight design (static-only
validated) as operational validation---not as the article's final result.
Authorised preflight execution and review must precede training. No claim of
effective learning, superiority, demonstrated novelty, or editorial acceptance
is made here.
