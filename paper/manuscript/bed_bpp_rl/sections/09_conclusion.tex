\section{Conclusion}

R02 delivers an implemented, synthetically verified BED-BPP-RL environment and
exportable experience format: a documented 36-dimensional observation, a
terminated/truncated step API, an atomic corpus contract with an agent-only
loader, and an independent verifier. Industrial corpus generation, training, and
computational characterisation remain subsequent authorised steps. No empirical
claim of policy improvement is made here.
