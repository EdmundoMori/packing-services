\section{Availability}

Design and implementation artefacts live under
\codepath{paper/studies/bed\_bpp\_rl/} and this manuscript tree. Source
dependencies are modules already present in packing-services; BED-BPP orders
remain external. No industrial experience corpus and no PPO checkpoint are
published in this preparation stage. The resource interface is intended to
support a later minimal CPU PPO run with new experiences and train/eval
separation after preflight review.
