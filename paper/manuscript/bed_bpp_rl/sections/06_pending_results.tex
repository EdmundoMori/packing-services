\section{Results and pending work}

\paragraph{Verified resource operation.}
The industrial preflight comprises four BED-BPP orders, two per target,
and four behaviour policies: three fixed rules and uniform rule selection.
The recovery completed 16 episodes and exported 648 transitions.
All episodes ended naturally; none was truncated.
Eight episodes placed the full sequence, whereas eight stopped when
the next item had no legal candidate.

The persisted episodes passed input, placement, geometry and return
checks. A copy of the corpus was verified from a different directory.
The recovery wall time was approximately 29.30 seconds.
The earlier failed attempt is preserved separately and contributes
approximately 3.56 seconds to the operational accounting.
It produced no confirmed packing episodes.

\paragraph{Scope of these results.}
These measurements demonstrate operation on four orders, not broad
coverage, tail latency, physical stability or effective RL learning.
Equal final volume does not imply identical plans.
Recorded identical RSS peaks are not interpreted as incremental
memory measurements for individual episodes.

\paragraph{Pending work.}
The initial corpus and a minimal CPU PPO demonstration remain pending.
The proposed design separates training and evaluation orders and
requires new on-policy experiences for PPO.
No claims of policy superiority, demonstrated novelty or editorial
acceptance are made.
Historical campaign results are not reused as results of this resource.
