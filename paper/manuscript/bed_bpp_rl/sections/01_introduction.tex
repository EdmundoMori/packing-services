\section{Introduction}

Online three-dimensional packing under industrial order streams requires
decision procedures that respect limited observability and computational
budgets. This study is organised around two explicit axes:
(i)~\emph{BED-BPP} industrial orders and targets as the packing domain, and
(ii)~\emph{reinforcement learning} (RL) as the interaction and learning
framework to be supported by a reusable resource---not as a claim of effective
learning already demonstrated.

BED-BPP provides realistic orders and targets~\citep{kagerer2023bedbpp},
while packing-configuration and extreme-point heuristics supply candidate
geometry~\citep{zhao2022pct,crainic2008extreme}. RL can, in principle, select
among such candidates or rules, but only if the environment interface makes
observations, actions, rewards, termination, and budgets explicit and
reproducible.

\paragraph{Question.}
How can BED-BPP orders be transformed into a reusable interaction and experience
resource for studying RL policies while keeping observability, actions, returns,
and compute budgets explicit?

\paragraph{Resource layers (kept distinct).}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A BED-BPP mono-container environment for RL interaction
    (reset/step/close; terminated vs truncated).
  \item A portable corpus of transitions with agent/audit separation and
    offline verification.
  \item A \emph{pending} minimal PPO demonstration on CPU, using \emph{new}
    experiences and an explicit train/evaluation split---not exporter unit
    tests and not heuristic baselines as a substitute for RL.
  \item Results and limitations actually verified so far (synthetic contractual
    integrity and designed preflight), without claiming learning efficacy,
    superiority, demonstrated novelty, or editorial acceptance.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Role of the real preflight.}
A measured preflight with fixed and seeded-uniform policies is an
\emph{operational} validation of cost, export, and reload on BED-BPP. It is
\emph{not} the final empirical result of the article. Training is not opened
here: the published preflight protocol must be executed and reviewed first.
