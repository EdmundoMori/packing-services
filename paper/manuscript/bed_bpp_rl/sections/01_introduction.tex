\section{Introduction}

Online three-dimensional packing under industrial order streams requires
decision procedures that respect limited observability and computational
budgets. BED-BPP provides realistic orders and targets~\citep{kagerer2023bedbpp},
while packing-configuration and extreme-point heuristics supply candidate
geometry~\citep{zhao2022pct,crainic2008extreme}. Reinforcement learning (RL)
can, in principle, select among such candidates or rules, but only if the
\emph{environment interface} makes observations, actions, rewards, termination,
and budgets explicit and reproducible.

\paragraph{Question.}
How can BED-BPP orders be transformed into a reusable interaction and experience
resource for studying RL policies while keeping observability, actions, returns,
and compute budgets explicit?

\paragraph{R01 contribution (design only).}
This manuscript drafts that resource---named BED-BPP-RL---by (i)~verifying which
repository components can be reused, (ii)~stating a minimal mono-container
rule-selection contract, and (iii)~defining a versioned transition schema.
No real orders are executed here; no models are trained; no experience files are
emitted. Novelty and venue fitness are not claimed.
