\section{Problem formulation}

We consider online packing of a single BED-BPP order into one container given by
the order target. Items arrive in the original sequence. At each decision the
environment exposes the current item and legal placements under containment and
non-overlap (rotation allowed; weight, basic stability, and load-bearing
inactive in the initial contract). The episode stops at the first item without a
legal candidate; remaining suffix items are recorded as unpacked for audit only.

\paragraph{Rule-selection MDP (initial resource).}
The agent selects among three fixed rule identities
\(\{\texttt{greedy\_best\_fit},\texttt{lowest\_top},\texttt{least\_height\_increase}\}\).
Identities are preserved even when two rules propose the same geometry. This
studies \emph{rule-selection} RL; it does not claim to represent every placement
policy (e.g.\ free indexing over the full legal list). A second action space is
out of scope for the initial resource.

\paragraph{Reward and return.}
The step reward is the incremental nominal placed volume divided by the bin
volume. With \(\gamma=1\), the undiscounted return on a complete
(non-truncated) episode equals geometric utilisation
\(U_{\mathrm{geom}}\) under this contract. Budget cuts mark
\texttt{truncated} without inventing a completing return.
