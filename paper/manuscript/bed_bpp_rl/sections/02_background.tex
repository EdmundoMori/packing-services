\section{Background}

BED-BPP~\citep{kagerer2023bedbpp} is treated as an \emph{external} order source:
targets and sequences motivate the problem, but this resource does not implement
the official robotic evaluation stack (Blender stability, full \(x_{\mathrm{kpi}}\)).
Zhao et al.~\citep{zhao2022pct} motivate online irrevocable placement; Crainic et
al.~\citep{crainic2008extreme} motivate extreme-point candidate generation.
Our generator is the repository's \emph{simplified} extreme-point engine after
maintenance correction C04, not a claim of completing Crainic's Algorithm~1 and
not the historical pre-C04 motor used in a closed learning-objectives campaign.

Prior internal pilots (compact geometric contract, rule-selection PPO, preference
labelling) supply reusable software patterns. Their frozen protocols, weights, and
result tables remain closed evidence and are not reopened here.
