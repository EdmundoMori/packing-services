\section{Resource design}

\paragraph{Environment contract (draft).}
Documented in \codepath{paper/studies/bed_bpp_rl/environment_contract_draft.json}:
original sequence; one target container; \(p=s=1\); up to six orientations;
containment and non-overlap; stop on first impossible item; present-only
36-dimensional observation (no suffix, future counts, or returns); incremental
volume reward; \(\gamma=1\). Each clause is checked against reusable
implementations (\codepath{build_compact_problem},
\codepath{RuleSelectionEnv}, \codepath{observation.py}, \codepath{rules.py}).

\paragraph{Corpus schema v1.}
Each stored transition separates \emph{agent-visible} fields
(observation, next observation, action, mask, reward, terminated, truncated)
from \emph{audit-only} fields (episode and step ids, order/target,
environment and engine versions, behaviour policy and seed, rule proposals,
chosen geometry, end reason, hashes, units). Auxiliary audit fields must not
reintroduce future information into training tensors.

\paragraph{Reuse stance.}
Import compatible modules; adapt wrappers under
\codepath{paper/studies/bed_bpp_rl/} rather than copying campaign trees; exclude
historical \(Q_{\hat{}}\) labels and closed-campaign captures as RL transitions
(see appendix).
