\section{Validation protocol (synthetic, executed)}

Success means a correct, reusable, RL-usable resource---not beating a heuristic.
R02 executes synthetic checks under
\codepath{paper/studies/bed\_bpp\_rl/tools/test\_r02\_synthetic.py}: all items
placed; first item impossible (zero transitions); next item impossible after a
valid placement; truncation with usable next observation/mask; natural
termination coinciding with the budget limit; coincident geometries with
preserved action identities; \texttt{step} after close; reset releasing the
previous episode; observation independence from the unseen suffix; reward versus
audited volume; interrupted writes, incomplete files, and duplicates; agent
loader excluding audit fields; and an explicit post-C04 engine probe. These
tests measure contractual correctness only; they are not performance on
BED-BPP. Real-order corpus sizing and wall budgets remain deferred to a measured
preflight.
