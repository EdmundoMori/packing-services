\section{Validation protocol (planned)}

Success means a correct, reusable, RL-usable resource---not beating a heuristic.
Planned small checks (not executed in R01): no future leakage in observations;
legal actions; reward sums equal \(U_{\mathrm{geom}}\) on complete episodes;
terminated versus truncated semantics; transition consistency; corpus
read/write; seeded reproducibility; a minimal on-policy update smoke test.
Corpus size and wall budgets are deferred until a measured preflight; they are
not extrapolated from closed campaigns here.
