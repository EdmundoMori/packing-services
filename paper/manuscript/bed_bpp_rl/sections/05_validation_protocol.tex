\section{Validation protocol (synthetic, executed)}

Success means a correct, reusable, RL-usable resource---not beating a heuristic.
R02 executed contractual synthetic checks; R02A adds integrity checks under
\codepath{test\_r02a\_integrity.py}: rejection of contradictory episode fields and
missing proposals; separation of structural validation from complete
geometry/volume audit; detection of post-confirmation file modification;
loading after moving the corpus directory; truncation without actions with a
usable bootstrap mask; policy callbacks denied audit channels; strict action and
budget types; and offline verification from persisted artifacts without a live
environment. These tests measure contractual integrity only; they are not
performance on BED-BPP. Real-order corpus sizing remains deferred to a measured
preflight.
