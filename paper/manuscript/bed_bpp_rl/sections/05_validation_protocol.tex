\section{Validation protocol (synthetic + designed preflight)}

Success means a correct, reusable resource on the BED-BPP and RL axes---not
beating a heuristic and not claiming learned policies. R02/R02A established
synthetic contractual integrity of the environment and transition corpus.
R03-preparation closes offline artifact contrast: unique input identities,
permitted orientations (without prefabricated acceptance), strict
placement/transition correspondence, per-step nominal-volume rewards, typed
unpacking/closure checks, and AABB containment/non-overlap with
\texttt{physical\_stability\_verified}=\texttt{null}. Missing fields yield
explicit not-checked statuses rather than silent passes. Adversarial synthetic
tests cover unknown ids, disallowed orientations, volume-preserving pose edits,
swapped placements, conserved-sum reward tampering, and insufficient snapshots.

A measured real preflight is \emph{designed} but not executed: four orders
(two targets; item-count quantiles), fixed and seeded-uniform policies
($\le 16$ episodes), explicit timeouts/wall/RSS/concurrency, portable corpus
reopen, and cost timers separated from \texttt{tracemalloc}/CPU. That preflight
is operational validation only. Four orders do not demonstrate latency tails.
Industrial corpus sizing for learning and the minimal CPU PPO demonstration
(with new experiences and train/eval separation) remain pending and must follow
preflight execution and review---not exporter tests or heuristics as substitutes.
