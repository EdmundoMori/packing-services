\section{Limitations}

The resource is mono-container and rule-discrete; it is not the official
BED-BPP robotic protocol and does not verify physical stability
(\texttt{physical\_stability\_verified}=\texttt{null}). Offline contrast
reports not-checked when snapshot fields are missing rather than inventing
passes. The designed real preflight uses four orders and does not demonstrate
latency tails; historical timings are orientation only. Absolute sample
independence is not claimed once orders are observed. Exporter and synthetic
tests do not constitute an RL demonstration. Novelty, venue fit, industrial
corpus size for learning, and PPO learning efficacy remain unset. Training is
not authorised until the published real preflight is executed and reviewed.
