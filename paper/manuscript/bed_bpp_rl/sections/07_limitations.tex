\section{Limitations}

The initial resource is mono-container and rule-discrete; it is not the official
BED-BPP robotic protocol and does not verify physical stability
(\texttt{physical\_stability\_verified}=\texttt{null}). The simplified
extreme-point engine is not proven complete relative to full extreme-point
literature. Historical campaigns used other motors or objectives; their numbers
must not be silently pooled with future BED-BPP-RL experiences. Dataset
redistribution is constrained by the external BED-BPP terms; this repository
records hashes and citations, not a redistributed dump. Novelty and venue fit
are unset.
