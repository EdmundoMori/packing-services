\section{Limitations}

The resource is mono-container and rule-discrete; it is not the official
BED-BPP robotic protocol and does not verify physical stability
(\texttt{physical\_stability\_verified}=\texttt{null}). The summarised
observation is not claimed to be Markov-sufficient. Manifest durability is
limited to atomic \texttt{fsync}+replace under a single writer; host crashes
between episode and manifest update can leave unconfirmed orphans. R02/R02A
evidence is synthetic contractual integrity only. Historical campaign numbers
must not be pooled with future BED-BPP-RL experiences. Novelty and venue fit
are unset. Real-order generation and training are not authorised by R02A.
