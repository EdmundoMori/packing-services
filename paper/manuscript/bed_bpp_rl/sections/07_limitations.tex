\section{Limitations}

The resource is mono-container and rule-discrete; it is not the official
BED-BPP robotic protocol and does not verify physical stability
(\texttt{physical\_stability\_verified}=\texttt{null}). The summarised
observation is not claimed to be Markov-sufficient. The simplified extreme-point
engine is not proven complete relative to full extreme-point literature.
R02 evidence is synthetic contractual correctness only. Historical campaigns
used other motors or objectives; their numbers must not be silently pooled with
future BED-BPP-RL experiences. Dataset redistribution is constrained by the
external BED-BPP terms; this repository records hashes and citations, not a
redistributed dump. Novelty and venue fit are unset. Real-order experience
generation and training are not authorised by R02.
