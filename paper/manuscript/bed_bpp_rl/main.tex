\begin{abstract}
We implement a reusable interaction resource that turns packing instances into
explicit online episodes for resource-constrained reinforcement learning
research. The contract uses a mono-container extreme-point session (post-C04),
a three-rule discrete action space, a 36-dimensional present-only observation,
and an incremental nominal-volume reward with \(\gamma=1\). Terminated and
truncated endings are separated, and experiences are exported under a
code-validated schema with an independent verifier. Evidence in this note is
contractual correctness on synthetic instances only; we do not train policies,
generate an industrial corpus, or claim novelty or benchmark superiority.
\end{abstract}
