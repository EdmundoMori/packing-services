\begin{abstract}
BED-BPP-RL is a reusable resource for resource-constrained reinforcement
learning research on industrial online packing. Its axes are BED-BPP orders and
an RL interaction contract: a mono-container extreme-point session (post-C04),
a three-rule discrete action space, a 36-dimensional present-only observation,
and an incremental nominal-volume reward with \(\gamma=1\), with terminated and
truncated endings separated and experiences exported under a verifiable schema.
This note reports synthetic contractual integrity and a designed measured
preflight; a minimal CPU PPO demonstration on new experiences with train/eval
separation remains pending and must not be replaced by exporter tests or
heuristics. We do not claim effective learning, superiority, demonstrated
novelty, or editorial acceptance.
\end{abstract}
