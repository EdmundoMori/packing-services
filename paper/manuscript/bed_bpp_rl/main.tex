\begin{abstract}
We outline a reusable interaction resource that turns industrial BED-BPP orders
into explicit online packing episodes for resource-constrained reinforcement
learning research. The initial contract reuses a mono-container extreme-point
session, a three-rule discrete action space, a 36-dimensional present-only
observation, and an incremental nominal-volume reward with \(\gamma=1\) so that
undiscounted returns match geometric utilisation on complete episodes.
Terminated and truncated endings are kept separate. This note records scope,
reuse, and a validation plan; it does not train policies, generate a corpus, or
claim novelty or benchmark superiority.
\end{abstract}
