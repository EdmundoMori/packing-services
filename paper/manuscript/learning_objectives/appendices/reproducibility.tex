\section{Reproducibility notes}

Artefacts live under
\texttt{paper/studies/counterfactual\_ranking/}
(closed exploratory pilot) and
\texttt{paper/studies/learning\_objectives/}
(frozen protocol, sample manifest, reduced preflight, labelling tools).
Manuscript sources are in
\texttt{paper/manuscript/learning\_objectives/}.

\paragraph{Campaign versus attempts.}
The frozen-protocol labelling \emph{campaign} admits multiple
\emph{attempts}. Attempt~01 wrote \texttt{learning\_labels/} and was interrupted
with unknown exit code/cause. Demonstrated facts include non-atomic
\texttt{write\_text} persistence, eleven empty JSON files under order
\texttt{00108806}, and a progress-versus-content inconsistency; the exact
corruption mechanism is not established. Progress is not the integrity source of
truth. Twelve train orders verify by on-disk content; fifty-nine orders remain
pending as registered in progress; development labels were not produced in
attempt~01. A provisional normalisation on the twelve intact orders is marked
non-usable for training.

\paragraph{Registered time and reserve.}
The sum of per-order \texttt{wall\_seconds} in progress
($214.80055754699697$\,s) is \emph{registered} time for sequential order work,
not a proven bound on total process wall time. A launcher artefact span
(progress versus execution-manifest mtimes, $\approx 216.7$\,s) provides a
verifiable interval between artefacts. An explicit operational reserve of
$120$\,s is applied inside the existing $13500$\,s labelling budget before
attempt~02; the budget is not expanded. Preflight
$35.121554053999716$\,s remains a separate, once-accounted phase.

\paragraph{Operational recovery layer.}
Atomic JSON writes (same-directory temp, flush/fsync, validate, \texttt{replace},
optional directory fsync), content-hash integrity, and attempt~02 execution are
implemented in \texttt{labeling\_io.py}, \texttt{labeling\_integrity.py},
\texttt{labeling\_campaign\_safe.py} and
\texttt{run\_learning\_labels\_recover.py}. No absolute durability against every
failure mode is claimed. The attempt~01 executor module is retained for
provenance. Forensic summaries are under \texttt{forensics/} and
\texttt{reviews/14\_interruption\_forensics.md}.

Complete labelling recovery, training and actor evaluation are documented
separately when executed. Seventeen untracked historical transition pickles
remain excluded from versioned claims.
