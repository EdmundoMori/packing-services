\section{Reproducibility notes}

Artefacts live under
\texttt{paper/studies/counterfactual\_ranking/}
(closed exploratory pilot) and
\texttt{paper/studies/learning\_objectives/}
(audits, frozen protocol, sample manifest, reduced preflight).
The manuscript sources are in
\texttt{paper/manuscript/learning\_objectives/}.
Frozen protocol and sample hashes are recorded in
\texttt{protocol\_frozen.json} and \texttt{sample\_manifest.json}.

Exploratory reanalysis code does not load checkpoints for inference and does
not call the packing engine as a confirmatory study. Historical campaign
closures are preserved. Seventeen untracked historical transition pickles are
intentionally excluded from versioned scientific claims. This draft does not
authorise labelling, training, development packing or test packing.
