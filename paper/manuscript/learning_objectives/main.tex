\begin{abstract}
This draft freezes a controlled empirical protocol comparing supervised learning
objectives for candidate selection in online one-container packing under a
shared geometric contract and a shared support map $S$. A reduced train-only
preflight is recorded; labelling, training, development and test are not
authorised here. The draft does not claim novelty, PCT superiority, physical
stability, or publication acceptance. An exploratory counterfactual pilot already
published in the repository is summarised separately and is not reused as a
result of this campaign.
\end{abstract}
