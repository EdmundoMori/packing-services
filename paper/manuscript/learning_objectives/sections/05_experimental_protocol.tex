\section{Experimental protocol}

\paragraph{Geometric contract.}
Original input order; one container of the order's target; lookahead and
selection widths $p=s=1$; at most six audited orientations; containment and
non-overlap active; weight, basic stability and load-bearing inactive; stop at
the first current item without a legal candidate; $U_{\mathrm{geom}}$ as above;
$\mathrm{physical\_stability\_verified}=\mathrm{null}$.

\paragraph{Support map.}
$S=\texttt{greedy\_plus\_orientation\_position\_diversity\_v1}$ is shared by
labelling, training and deployment. It keeps at most four distinct geometries,
always includes greedy first, then maximises the lexicographic key
$(\mathrm{new\_orientation},\min$ Chebyshev distance to chosen,
$-\mathrm{source\_index})$ after exact floating identities.

\paragraph{Eligible choice states and quantiles.}
Along a greedy trajectory, a time step is an \emph{eligible choice state} when
$|\mathcal{L}_t|\ge 2$ and greedy returns a legal action. Let $N$ be the number
of eligible choice states. Up to $L=4$ states are retained at quantile indices
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{floor}\!\left(i\,\frac{N-1}{L-1}+\tfrac12\right),\qquad i=0,\ldots,L-1,
\end{equation}
when $N>L$; if $N\le L$ all eligible states are kept. Duplicate indices are
dropped without refill. Incomplete labelled states (any unknown $Q_{\mathrm{hat}}$)
are retained for audit but excluded from normalisation and training.

Sample sizes are a scope and budget decision, not a power analysis. Labels are
built on greedy trajectories; deployment induces a different state distribution,
which is an intentional transfer gap rather than a hidden confounder.

\paragraph{Label construction.}
Labels are continuation returns $Q_{\mathrm{hat}}$ under greedy packing after each
candidate in $S_t$, with unknown returns recorded as null. Sixteen preflight
continuations are reusable at most once when hashes and captures match, counted
globally once across attempts. The labelling campaign may comprise multiple
attempts under the same frozen protocol. Attempt~01 was interrupted with unknown
cause and exit code; twelve train orders remain content-verified for reuse;
order \texttt{00108806} is corrupt on disk and requires an inevitable repeat for
insufficient evidence; development labels and remaining train orders were pending
after attempt~01. A provisional normalisation on the partial train subset is not
used for training. Operational forensics and recovery mechanics are deferred to
the appendix and study reviews. Label evidence is not actor evaluation.

\paragraph{Contrasts, gate and budget.}
Primary: order-level mean, after averaging paired seeds, of
$U_{\mathrm{geom}}(\mathrm{preferences})-U_{\mathrm{geom}}(\mathrm{classification})$.
Secondary: return-difference versus each main arm; both main arms versus greedy;
target breakdowns; $R_S$; failures and timings. The development gate
(mean preference margin $\ge 0.005$, positive in at least two of three seeds,
non-negative aggregate on each target, complete audit, remaining test budget) is
an engineering continuity rule, not a significance test, and does not require
beating greedy. Wall-clock ceilings allocate 900\,s preflight, 13500\,s
labelling, 1800\,s training, 3600\,s development and 9000\,s test within a
28800\,s experimental compute cap; writing time is separate. Labelling dominates
cost because each alternative requires a continuation, capture and audit.
Training and evaluation budgets remain undemonstrated until those stages run.
