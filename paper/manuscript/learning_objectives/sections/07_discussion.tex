\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{Primary contrast and Greedy reference.}
On the frozen development sample, episode-level mean
preferences$-$classification was $-0.00808$ after averaging paired seeds within
each order and then averaging orders with equal weight.
Both learned arms trailed GreedyBestFit (preferences$-$Greedy $\approx -0.0516$;
classification$-$Greedy $\approx -0.044$). These readouts describe this
configuration only; they do not refute preference learning in general.
Three seeds are not three independent orders: seed variation is nested inside
orders and then collapsed before the order-level mean.

\paragraph{Exploratory pilot versus this campaign.}
An earlier counterfactual pilot (different study, smaller development sample,
different support contract) reported an approximate positive
preferences$-$classification margin. That pilot is not reproduced here: sample
sizes, $S$, and episode contract differ. Attributing the sign change to any
single factor (loss, target, Adam steps, or support) would be unwarranted.

\paragraph{Labels, states and geometry.}
Continuation returns $Q_{\mathrm{hat}}$ supervise local actions on greedy
trajectories; deployment actors visit a different state distribution. Restricting
scoring to $S_t$ (at most four candidates) is a budgeted deployment choice, not
an claim that the full legal list was scored. Loss magnitudes are not comparable
across objectives; equal wall-clock training does not imply equal convergence.
Uncertainty combines order sampling and three paired seeds---seeds are averaged
within orders, not treated as independent orders.

\paragraph{Scope and missing test.}
Results apply to twenty-four development orders and two BED-BPP targets under the
compact contract. The preregistered test split remains closed under the failed
continuity gate; out-of-sample generalisation is therefore \emph{not claimed}.
Closing the test does not by itself make the development report incoherent, nor
is opening it proposed here to enlarge publication claims. Physical stability
was not verified and is not part of the reported AABB utilisation claims.

\paragraph{Operational incident.}
An initial development launch failed at the evaluator harness (venv interpreter
resolution) before any packing capture; it is documented separately and excluded
from algorithmic contrasts. It is not the scientific contribution of this work.
