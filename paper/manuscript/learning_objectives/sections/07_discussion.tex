\section{Discussion}

The exploratory pilot suggests that the supervision objective can matter for
complete-episode utilisation even when architecture and labels are shared, but
it does not establish a general ranking among learning methods, statistical
equivalence, or improvement over greedy placement. The candidate-subset
mismatch and privileged greedy continuations limit causal attribution.

Mandi et al.'s losses clarify how a return-difference objective could be
specified, while also marking why the existing preference loss should not be
renamed as their pairwise-difference surrogate.

Closed campaigns on historical actors, compact selectors, and PPO rule
selection remain out of scope for pooling. They only bound expectations about
cost and failure modes.
