\section{Discussion}

Several issues must be addressed once the frozen campaign reports numbers.
Continuation returns $Q_{\mathrm{hat}}$ supervise local actions but are not the
return of the learned actor; improvement on labelled greedy states need not
transfer to states visited under $\pi_\theta$. Restricting decisions to $S_t$
trades coverage for budget and can leave high-value legal placements unscored.
The three losses operate on different numeric scales; a common Adam schedule
equalises optimisation effort, not loss magnitude. Uncertainty arises from both
order sampling and seed pairing. Finally, the geometric contract and the two
BED-BPP targets bound external validity: results must not be read as robotic
stability certificates or as comparisons against PCT.
