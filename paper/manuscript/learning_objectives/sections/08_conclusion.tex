\section{Conclusion}

This manuscript specifies a controlled comparison of supervision objectives for
online packing candidate selection under a shared geometric contract and support
map. Differentiable realisations of the frozen losses and a labelling executor
are in place. Labelling under the frozen protocol was interrupted with partial
content-verified evidence; a recovery path is designed but not executed.
Definitive empirical conclusions remain pending until complete labelling,
training and evaluation are authorised and finished. No claim of superiority,
physical stability or publication readiness is made here.
