\section{Conclusion}

This manuscript specifies a controlled comparison of supervision objectives for
online packing candidate selection under a shared geometric contract and support
map. Differentiable realisations of the frozen losses and a labelling executor
are in place; definitive empirical conclusions remain pending until the
preregistered labelling, training and evaluation stages are authorised and
completed. No claim of superiority, physical stability or publication readiness
is made here.
