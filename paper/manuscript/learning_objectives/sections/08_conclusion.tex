\section{Conclusion}

Under the executed frozen configuration---shared geometry, encoder, support map
$S$, labels, and optimisation budget---paired preference learning did \emph{not}
outperform classification on the primary development contrast
(preferences$-$classification mean $\approx -0.00808$), and neither learned arm
outperformed GreedyBestFit on episode $U_{\mathrm{geom}}$. The preregistered
development gate failed; the test split was not evaluated. We therefore close
this configuration as \texttt{no\_avanzar\_con\_esta\_configuracion}.

This closure answers the stated question for one controlled setup. It does not
establish impossibility of better learned selectors, superiority over PCT, or
physical stability. Any broader claim would require different authorised evidence,
not retrospective relabelling of this campaign.
