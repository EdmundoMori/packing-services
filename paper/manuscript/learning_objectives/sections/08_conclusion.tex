\section{Conclusion}

Under the executed frozen configuration---shared geometry, encoder, support map
$S$, labels, and optimisation budget---paired preference learning did \emph{not}
outperform classification on the primary development contrast
(preferences$-$classification mean $\approx -0.00808$), and neither learned arm
outperformed GreedyBestFit on episode $U_{\mathrm{geom}}$. The preregistered
development gate failed; the test split was not evaluated. We therefore close
this configuration as \codepath{no_avanzar_con_esta_configuracion}.

This closure answers the stated question for one controlled development setup.
It does not establish impossibility of better learned selectors, superiority over
PCT, or physical stability. Novelty and venue fitness are not established here.
Any broader generalisation claim would require separately authorised evidence,
not retrospective relabelling of this campaign or reopening the closed test to
``rescue'' publication.
