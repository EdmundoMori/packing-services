\section{Related work}

Decision-focused learning trains predictors so that downstream combinatorial
decisions have low regret rather than low parameter error alone.
\cite{mandi2022decision} cast that setting as learning to rank feasible
solutions and derive pointwise, pairwise, pairwise-difference, and listwise
surrogate losses over a subset $S$ of solutions. Their pairwise-difference loss
regresses differences of true objective values against differences of predicted
objectives (their Eq.~13). Their listwise loss follows a ListNet-style cross-entropy with a temperature,
as stated in Mandi et al.\ (their Eq.~15--16).

Our setting differs in structural ways documented in the repository audit:
candidates are local packing actions rather than full CO solutions; labels are
returns of a concrete greedy continuation rather than solver-optimal objective
values; and the deployed scorer selects among candidates at each online step.
We therefore treat Mandi et al.\ as a source of adaptable loss forms, not as a
target for exact reproduction.
