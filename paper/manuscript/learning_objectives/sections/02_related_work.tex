\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Packing and candidate generation.}
Constructive three-dimensional packing relies on a placement rule that proposes
candidate locations among the free space created by already packed items. Extreme
points are a classical mechanism for generating such locations and for deriving
fast heuristics \citep{crainic2008extreme}. Online learned packing instead
scores a structured action set with a policy network; Zhao et al.\ introduce
packing configuration trees (PCT) and train deep reinforcement learning policies
over tree leaves \citep{zhao2022pct}. Our engine likewise exposes a finite legal
list, then restricts scoring to a deterministic support map $S$ of at most four
geometries. We do not claim identity with extreme-point FFD/BFD, nor do we
evaluate PCT experimentally.

\paragraph{Learning of placement selection.}
Prior work on learned online packing emphasises representation and exploration of
the action space under reinforcement learning \citep{zhao2022pct}. The present
study holds the representation and packing engine fixed and varies only the
supervised objective used to fit a shared multilayer scorer. The setting is
therefore complementary: it isolates supervision rather than proposing a new
search architecture.

\paragraph{Decision-focused and ranking objectives.}
Decision-focused learning trains predictors so that downstream decisions improve,
not only predictive accuracy. Mandi et al.\ connect that agenda to learning-to-rank
losses and, in particular, to regressing pairwise score differences onto pairwise
solution-value differences \citep{mandi2022decision}. We adapt that pairwise
difference idea to local packing candidates labelled by greedy continuations.
Our existing preference loss remains a softplus ranking surrogate and is
\emph{not} Mandi's regress-on-difference objective.

\paragraph{Data source and evaluation scope.}
BED-BPP provides realistic robotic packing orders and an evaluation protocol that
includes stability simulation \citep{kagerer2023bedbpp}. We use BED-BPP as an
instance source. Episode quality is measured by geometric utilisation under the
compact contract (containment and non-overlap active; weight, basic stability and
load-bearing inactive; stop at the first current item without a legal candidate).
That contract must not be conflated with Zhao's PCT protocol or with Kagerer's
official robotic benchmark.
