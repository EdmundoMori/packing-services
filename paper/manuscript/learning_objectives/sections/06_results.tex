\section{Results}

\subsection{Exploratory pilot}

The closed counterfactual pilot compared classification and preference losses
with shared data and architecture on twelve development orders and three seeds
(84 complete episodes). Recalculation from published per-order results gives:
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{preferences}-\mathrm{classification} &= 0.03094863126240079,\\
\mathrm{preferences}-\mathrm{greedy} &= -0.019783628885582008,\\
\mathrm{classification}-\mathrm{greedy} &= -0.050732260147982794.
\end{align*}
An analysis designed after observing that pilot, using order-level means and a
stratified order bootstrap (20{,}000 replicates, seed 20261004), yields an
exploratory 95\% interval for the preferences-minus-classification mean of
$[-0.004208279906580691, 0.07644716396432695]$. The interval includes zero and
is not a confirmatory claim.

That pilot labelled at most four candidates per state while deployment scored
the full legal list. It is therefore exploratory and not an exact reproduction
of the unified support contract $S$ specified in this draft. Ranking regret on
labelled greedy states did not uniformly foreshadow episode-level utilisation.
