\section{Results}

\subsection{Exploratory pilot (not this campaign)}

A closed counterfactual pilot compared classification and preference losses with
shared data and architecture on twelve development orders and three seeds
(84 complete episodes). Recalculation from published per-order artefacts yields
approximate means
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{preferences}-\mathrm{classification} &\approx 0.031,\\
\mathrm{preferences}-\mathrm{greedy} &\approx -0.020,\\
\mathrm{classification}-\mathrm{greedy} &\approx -0.051.
\end{align*}
Exact values are archived with the pilot. An analysis designed after observing
that pilot, using order-level means and a stratified order bootstrap
(20{,}000 replicates), produced an exploratory 95\% interval for the
preferences-minus-classification mean of approximately $[-0.004,\,0.076]$. The
interval includes zero and is not confirmatory. That pilot labelled at most four
candidates while deployment scored the full legal list, so it is not an exact
reproduction of the unified support contract $S$ used here.

\subsection{Label generation under the frozen protocol}

Labelling completed for the protocol-permitted train and development sample
after three attempts. Coverage is $48$ train and $24$ development orders
($24$/$12$ per target); test was not executed. Attempt~01 left twelve
content-verified train orders; attempt~02 reused them read-only, repeated
\texttt{00108806}, and verified additional train labels before interrupting;
attempt~03 referenced forty-six verified orders and executed the twenty-six
pending ones only. Independent verification reports zero unknown $Q_{\mathrm{hat}}$,
unique $(\mathrm{order},\mathrm{state},\mathrm{action})$ keys (the same geometry
may recur across states), sixteen preflight continuations reused once, a final
train-only normalisation ($743$ equal-weight rows) and $372$ development learning
rows unused for fitting. Continuation returns
are supervision artefacts only, not primary packing or online-improvement
findings; descriptive margins belong in study reviews with explicit denominators.
