\section{Results}

\subsection{Exploratory pilot (not this campaign)}

A closed counterfactual pilot compared classification and preference losses with
shared data and architecture on twelve development orders and three seeds
(84 complete episodes). Recalculation from published per-order artefacts yields
approximate means
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{preferences}-\mathrm{classification} &\approx 0.031,\\
\mathrm{preferences}-\mathrm{greedy} &\approx -0.020,\\
\mathrm{classification}-\mathrm{greedy} &\approx -0.051.
\end{align*}
Exact values are archived with the pilot. An analysis designed after observing
that pilot, using order-level means and a stratified order bootstrap
(20{,}000 replicates), produced an exploratory 95\% interval for the
preferences-minus-classification mean of approximately $[-0.004,\,0.076]$. The
interval includes zero and is not confirmatory. That pilot labelled at most four
candidates while deployment scored the full legal list, so it is not an exact
reproduction of the unified support contract $S$ used here.

\subsection{Paired training under the frozen protocol}

Nine models were fitted once (three arms $\times$ seeds $11,23,37$) with paired
initialisation, shared per-seed permutations, and forty Adam updates per model.
Each update is one full-batch gradient step on the arithmetic mean of the $192$
train labelled states (protocol string: one step per epoch). Checkpoints record
\texttt{n\_optimizer\_steps}${}=40$. This is \emph{not} minibatch SGD: permuting
the order of a sum does not create stochastic mini-batches. Final checkpoints are
epoch~$40$ only; no development-based selection was applied. Wall budget for
training is $1800$\,s and was not expanded; a common update count does not imply
equal wall-clock or equal convergence across arms. Loss magnitudes are not
comparable across objectives and do not rank packing quality. An earlier launch
under commit \texttt{2176460} aborted at frozen-hash validation of
\texttt{model\_spec.py} before any data load or Adam step; the valid run started
from fresh initialisations after restore commit \texttt{468ff51}.

\subsection{Development episodes (this campaign)}

\paragraph{Operational harness incident (not packing).}
An earlier launch wrote $240$ keys but every worker crashed before capture when
\texttt{Path.resolve()} followed the venv interpreter symlink to the system
Python. That artefact remains under \texttt{learning\_development\_eval/} with its
original zeros; it is not a packing evaluation and does not enter the contrasts
below.

\paragraph{Authorised development evaluation.}
After the interpreter fix, an eight-episode smoke and then a full development
campaign ($24$ orders; $24$ GreedyBestFit on the full legal list $+216$
support-constrained actors) completed with $240$ unique audited captures. Eight
smoke keys were integrated once by hash-compatible reuse; $232$ keys were newly
executed. Aggregation: mean across seeds within each order, then equal-weight
mean across orders. Tie tolerance for win/tie/loss counts is
$10^{-9}$ (\texttt{TIE\_EPS}). Test was not opened.

three hold here.
