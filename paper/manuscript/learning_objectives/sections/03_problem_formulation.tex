\section{Problem formulation}

An order is a fixed sequence of items together with a single container of a
declared target (euro-pallet or rollcontainer). At decision time $t$ the engine
exposes the current item $i_t$ and the legal candidate set $\mathcal{L}_t$ under
the compact geometric contract: containment and non-overlap are enforced;
rotations follow the audited orientation conversion (at most six permutations);
weight limits, basic stability and load-bearing are inactive; packing stops when
the current item has no legal candidate. Physical stability is recorded as
$\mathrm{null}$.

A candidate encoder maps the present session state and a candidate $a\in\mathcal{L}_t$
to a 17-dimensional feature vector that excludes future suffix identifiers and
return labels. Before scoring, every arm applies the same pure support map
\begin{equation}
  S_t = S(\mathcal{L}_t,a^{\mathrm{greedy}}_t)\subseteq\mathcal{L}_t,
\end{equation}
with $|S_t|\le 4$, $a^{\mathrm{greedy}}_t\in S_t$, deterministic diversification
by orientation and Chebyshev distance, and no access to $Q_{\mathrm{hat}}$.
Labelling, training and deployment call this single implementation.

A scorer $f_\theta$ assigns a scalar score $s_a=f_\theta(x_a)$ to each
$a\in S_t$. Larger scores are better; exact logit ties keep the smallest index in
$S_t$. The induced policy is
\begin{equation}
  \pi_\theta(t)=\arg\max_{a\in S_t} s_a
\end{equation}
with that tie-break. Episode quality is geometric utilisation
\begin{equation}
  U_{\mathrm{geom}}
  =
  \frac{\sum_{p\in\mathrm{placements}}\mathrm{vol}(p)}{\mathrm{vol}(\mathrm{container})}.
\end{equation}

Privileged continuation returns used only when building labels are
\begin{equation}
  Q_{\mathrm{hat}}(t,a)
  =
  U_{\mathrm{geom}}\bigl(\mathrm{GreedyContinuation}(t,a)\bigr),
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the utilisation obtained after committing $a$ and completing the remaining
suffix with greedy. They are not global optima and not values of $\pi_\theta$.
Support regret on labelled states,
\begin{equation}
  R_S(t)=\max_{a\in S_t} Q_{\mathrm{hat}}(t,a)-Q_{\mathrm{hat}}(t,\pi(t)),
\end{equation}
is diagnostic only when every needed return is known and $\pi(t)\in S_t$; it does
not replace complete-episode evaluation.
